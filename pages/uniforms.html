Civil Air Patrol Uniform Guide

Official uniform specifications, Vanguard item references, and compliance guidelines for Cadets and Senior Officers in accordance with CAPR 39-1. Correct wear of all authorized uniforms is expected during official Civil Air Patrol activities.

Cadet Uniform Vouchers

When registering, cadets may choose to receive a Vanguard uniform voucher issued immediately upon their first promotion. Financial assistance levels are set nationally—the local squadron has no control over voucher amounts.

Prospective / New Cadets

New cadets without a full uniform wear Squadron Business Casual: Plain white button-down or polo shirt, black trousers/pants, black socks, and plain black shoes.

Accessories & Gear Rule

Per CAPR 39-1, all back packs, hydration packs, jackets/coats, and watches carried or worn with uniforms must be solid black with no commercial logos or printing.

Senior Officer Uniform Requirements

Mandatory grooming and wear standards for adult Senior Members

Mandatory Grooming & Weight Standards

Wear of Air Force style uniforms (Blues, ABUs, OCPs) by Senior Members is strictly subject to the height/weight and grooming standards set forth in CAPR 39-1 without exception. Members not meeting AF-style standards should wear Corporate-style uniforms (Aviator, Polo, Corporate Field).
