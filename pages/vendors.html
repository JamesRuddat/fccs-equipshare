Uniform Compliance Notice

Members are strictly responsible for ensuring that all clothing, insignia, and equipment purchased from third-party or surplus vendors fully comply with CAPR 39-1 (CAP Uniform Regulation) prior to wear during official Civil Air Patrol activities.
